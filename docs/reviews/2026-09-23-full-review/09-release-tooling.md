### Cross-cutting findings
1. **Destructive git ops lack a shared safety primitive.** prune-remote (remote delete), worktree-lib release (`branch -D`), wrap-ticket's legacy path (`branch -D`) and train-lib restore (`reset --hard`) each carry their own guard, or none. Only prune-remote has an ancestor/cherry check, and it checks the wrong ref.
2. **Portability.** No platform floor is documented, and doctor does not probe for one. Bash 4 is needed by 8 scripts (check-ship-history, reconcile, epic-lib, dag-lib, among others), `flock` by 7 (sidecar, task-store, epic-lib, …), and GNU `timeout` by 5 (poll.sh, among others). All are invoked as `bash X`, which is `/bin/bash` 3.2 on macOS.
3. **CI parity.** The only bite tests in CI are bump-class, prune-remote, plugin-dir, sync-includes, retro-gate and seed-pack. That is how release-train/test-integration went red unnoticed. `/release` Steps 4.6 (council template variables) and 4.7 (hook templates) are also not in CI.
4. **Temp-file pattern.** `mktemp "$TMPDIR"` + `mv` into the repo, used across train-lib, close.sh and reconcile, is non-atomic across filesystems and leaves files at mode 0600. poll.sh uses predictable fixed `/tmp` names.
5. **Spec↔implementation drift.** SPEC-016 no-force vs worktree-lib L325. SPEC-023's "user must re-register" is impossible under M15. `release/SKILL.md` says C1–C4 while C5 exists. SPEC-032 says D1–D8 while the SKILL says D1–D10. audit's TTY confirm is documented but not implemented. audit's must-split 40KB is documented but not enforced.
6. **Dead code.** `check-ship-history.sh:373`, `train-lib.sh:776-778`, `plugin-dir.sh:146,290`, `audit.sh` `SKILL_HARD_BYTES`.
